# Spec: grounded_qa (v0.1)

**Goal.** Answer a question from supplied passages and cite the section that supports the
answer.

## Inputs
- `sections.parquet` rows with `snapshot_date == as_of`, `reserved == False`, and a
  non-empty `text`.
- Paragraphs come from `sections.paragraphs` (JSON: `label_path`, `labeled`, `kind`, `text`,
  `defined_term`).

## Two sources of base questions

### A. Definition templates (`question_source = "template"`, no LLM)
For each paragraph with `defined_term` set, in a section whose heading contains "Definitions":
- The question asks what the term means, in the agency's context, without naming the section.
  Templates `def-1` … `def-5`, for example:
  - `def-1`: "Under {agency_short} regulations on {part_topic}, what does the term "{term}" mean?"
  - `def-3`: "How do the {part_topic} rules define "{term}"?"
- `{part_topic}` comes from the part heading in lowercase, with the "PART nnn—" prefix stripped.
- Gold `answer` is the definition text with the leading term and "means" removed. Gold
  `citations` is `[title CFR section]`, plus the paragraph path when the paragraph is labeled.
- Skip terms whose definition is only a cross-reference ("is defined at § 122.23"). Those
  become abstention near-miss material instead.
- Cap: at most 8 definitions per section, sampled with the seed.

### B. LLM-written questions (`question_source = "llm"`)
For labeled paragraphs with 25–400 words that contain a requirement (regex on
`shall|must|may not|is required to|no later than|within \d+ days`):
- The question writer gets one paragraph plus its section heading. It must return JSON
  `{question, answer, answer_span}`, where `answer_span` is a verbatim substring of the paragraph.
- Reject the output if `answer_span` is not a substring, if the question names the section or
  label, or if the question shares more than 8 consecutive tokens with the paragraph.
- Gold `citations`: `[title CFR section + label_path]`. `acceptable_citations`: `[title CFR section]`.
- `question_model` is recorded. **The judge model in eval must be a different model family.**
- Cap: at most 3 questions per section.

## Conditions
- `gold_only`: the gold section passage (full section text, truncated to 1,500 tokens
  centered on the gold paragraph if longer; the truncation is marked with "…").
- `gold_distractors`: gold plus 3 BM25 distractors, shuffled.
- The same `base_id` is used for both conditions. The no-gold variant of the same base question
  is generated by the abstention task, so the three conditions can be compared per question.

## Metrics (eval)
- Answer correctness: the judge scores 0/1 against the gold answer. Template items are also
  scored by token-F1 against the definition.
- Citation precision and recall at section level. Paragraph-level precision is reported
  separately.
- Support: each answer sentence must be entailed by a cited passage (automatic check).
